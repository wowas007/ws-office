3. Data collection on this website

Server log files

When you visit this website, information is automatically stored in server log files. This includes: IP address, date and time of the request, URL accessed, browser type and operating system. Processing is carried out on the basis of Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and error-free provision of the website).

Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, your details (e-mail address, name, message) will be stored for the purpose of processing the enquiry. The data will not be passed on without your consent and will be deleted after final processing, unless statutory retention obligations preclude this. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR.

Web analytics with Matomo

This website uses Matomo (formerly Piwik), an open-source software for the statistical analysis of website visits. Matomo runs on a dedicated server at the German hosting provider Strato AG (Otto-Ostrowski-Strasse 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany). Each time this website is accessed, the data required for tracking processing is transmitted to this Strato server. No further transfer to third parties takes place. Further information: strato.de/datenschutz.

The following data is processed during analysis: the anonymised IP address, pages accessed, date and time of access, browser type and operating system, and the referring page (referrer). The IP address is anonymised before storage, so that no conclusions can be drawn about individual users.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in the anonymised analysis of user behaviour for the improvement of the website).
