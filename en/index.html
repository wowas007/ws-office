Wolfgang Schmidt former Federal Minister

Hamburger in Berlin. Father. Builder of compromise. Transatlanticist. Modernizer of government. Security policy expert.

Wolfgang Schmidt is among Germany's most experienced political strategists and has built a unique network across politics, business, and society. He has shaped politics at the highest level for over 25 years – in Berlin and Hamburg, in Europe and internationally: as Federal Minister, State Secretary, and State Councillor.

Today, he brings his experience in government and politics into public debate – as a speaker, adviser, and thought leader.

For many years, he has worked on central future issues: from economic, financial, and industrial policy to labour and social affairs and the modernisation of the state – with a special focus on digitalisation and cutting red tape. After Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, Schmidt contributed his foreign and security policy expertise to shaping Germany's "Zeitenwende" and supported Chancellor Olaf Scholz in redefining German policy.
